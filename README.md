# Mayo Clinic: Building the Platform for the Future of Medicine

An HBR-style MBA case study analyzing Mayo Clinic's digital transformation strategy under Dr. John Halamka — developed for ITEC-617 (Digital Transformation) at Kogod School of Business, American University, Spring 2026.

## What this project is

This is a complete four-document case study package examining how Mayo Clinic transformed from a 157-year-old clinical institution into an AI-powered healthcare platform through the creation of Mayo Clinic Platform. The protagonist is Dr. John Halamka, the inaugural president of Mayo Clinic Platform, who built the unit during the COVID-19 crisis.

The case examines the strategic tension between protecting a trusted brand and moving fast enough to matter in a winner-take-most platform market. It covers data governance, de-identification at scale, platform network effects, and the ethics of monetizing patient data for algorithmic research.

## Key analytical themes

- Platform strategy in regulated industries (healthcare, data privacy, HIPAA)
- Building internal AI capabilities vs. partnering with hyperscalers (Google, Microsoft)
- Monetizing institutional data assets without eroding institutional trust
- Organizational design for innovation in a legacy institution
- Crisis as a forcing function for digital transformation
